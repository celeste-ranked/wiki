# Gameplay

Celeste Ranked in its core is a 1v1. Two players race on same randomly generated sequence of rooms (check out the original [randomizer mod](https://gamebanana.com/tools/6848)), drawn from vanilla Celeste and a pool of approved custom maps. First to finish wins.

Celeste Ranked is quite different from normal ranked based systems in games in the sense that different matches do not have equal playing fields. Unlike in ranked games like chess or TETR.IO, where the board state and rules are fixed regardless of the skill of the players, Celeste Ranked calibrates the room pool you receive based on your ELO.

Due to the nature of Celeste, a room that is trivial for a high ELO player may be practically impossible for a less skilled low ELO player, due to requiring entirely different movement tech that a lower ranked player has not learned and cannot execute competitively. Since the skill ceiling in Celeste is so extreme and involves fundamentally different movement tech rather than just harder execution, Celeste Ranked tailors the rooms you are given and tech you are expected to perform based on your ELO. This allows for each Celeste Ranked match between players of similar ELO to be a genuinely competitive race where both players are capable of completing each room.

This makes Celeste Ranked similar to a system like Osu! Ranked, where maps and their difficulties are picked as per your level, but the system is more heavily enforced in Celeste Ranked.
