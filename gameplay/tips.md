
Collected from the Celeste Ranked Discord, here are a few general tips to make your experience just a tad smoother!

# In-Match

- If both you and your opponent are stuck at a room, you can use the "/skip" command in chat to propose to skip the room. If your opponent accepts, then both of you will be teleported to the next time. It is only usggested to use this if you are both really stuck - see the next section for practicing that room!

- Maps will **sometimes force you to backtrack**. This can either be because the main path forward is blocked by clutter blocks (the 3A huge mess blocks), or by a key. If you find a key or clutter switch in a dead-end room, **start going back through rooms until you can use it!**

- Keys can only spawn in their original locations, or in place of berries. They will always have yellow particles around them, even if hidden behind a seeker block or fake wall. Also, because you don't need ot reach safe ground before collecting a key, feel free to death-warp right after you have touched the key!

- Room exits will always have a lightbeam effect emanating from them. Additionally, if a room has 3 different exits/entrances and is larger than a screen, there will be an arrow surrounding madeline pointing towards the correct exit. This even works with backtracking rooms!

- Feel free to disable the HUD if you feel like you are stressing out about your opponent's progress. You can do this under the "Options" menu in the pause menu or start screen, or using a keybind (default is F3) in a ranked match.

- If you have the HUD enabled, at the bottom of your screen the current room name will be displayed. If there is a "-m" suffix to the end of the room name, that means that the room is *mirrored* compared to the original. This can be useful if the room is almost symmetrical at first glance, but has some asymmetry (pretty much every backwards summit badeline room).


# Post-Match

Replays are **PARAMOUNT** to your success as a player. By opening up a replay, you can watch your opponent's routing through a room, and even practice rooms you were stuck on! The next few tips will be dedicated to effective replay usage.

- You can open up **any** replays, not just ones from your own matches (within 3 days after the match is played). You can do this by searching up a player's name in the leaderboard (Journal), and going to Matches->(match you want to watch)->Watch Replay

- You can practice any room directly from the replay by hitting the specified keybind (default is P). This will teleport you to the nearest spawnpoint from the ghost currently playing the room.

- While in practice mode, you can hit F6 to open the debug map. This is useful for a couple of reasons: firstly, if you were confused during a game about where to go (due to some complex backtrackign shenanigans), the debug map helps show all room and path locations. But also, by Shift+Clicking on a position in a room in debug mode, you can **change your spawn to that location within the room**. This can be useful for practicing specific bits of a room without playing through the whole room. Note that this doesn't work in rooms where you have to collect things throughout the whole room (like badeline orb rooms).
